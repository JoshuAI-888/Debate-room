# Principles

- Reliability is a foundation of earned trust (S001, first additional prescription; S003, sloth and unreliability).
- Learn vicariously rather than repeating predecessors' mistakes (S001, second prescription).
- Continue learning across disciplines (S002, opening; S003, wisdom acquisition).
- Avoid ideology that prevents hearing contrary arguments (S002, ideology discussion; S003, iron prescription).
- Separate competence from performance of competence (S003, Planck/chauffeur story).
- Evaluate the system that rewards behavior, not just participants' stated intentions (S003, perverse incentives).
These are paraphrased heuristics from the speeches, not empirical guarantees.
