# Frameworks

## Inversion (S001; S002; S003)
Ask what would make this option fail. List concrete failure conditions and work backward to prevention. Do not confuse avoiding failure with eliminating all risk. Application to staged business tests is an inference.

## Latticework (S002)
Combine relevant models across disciplines: incentives, probabilities, compounding, and operational reality. Explain the causal mechanism; naming models is not analysis. Locator: opening discussion of the latticework, then the bridge example.

## Incentive audit (S003)
Who receives money, status, or protection from each option? How could that reward distort reporting or behavior? Locator: paragraphs beginning Another thing: perverse incentives and discussion of self-serving bias.

## Disconfirmation and checklist (S001; S003)
Write the strongest case against the favored option, specify evidence that would change the recommendation, and review the material risks before voting. Locator: Darwin discussion in S001 and Objectivity maintenance in S003.
